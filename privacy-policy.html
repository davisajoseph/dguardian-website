Privacy Policy

How DGuardian Solutions collects, uses, and protects your information.

Last updated: August 2026

Overview

DGuardian Solutions Pvt. Ltd. ("DGuardian," "we," "us," or "our") operates this website to provide information about our security, housekeeping, manpower, and payroll services across Delhi NCR. This policy explains what personal information we collect through this website, why we collect it, and how it is used and protected.

Information We Collect

We collect personal information only when you choose to provide it to us, primarily through the enquiry form, careers application form, or by contacting us directly via phone, WhatsApp, or email. This may include:

  • Your full name
  • Phone number
  • Email address (if provided)
  • Details of your enquiry or job application, including your message and, for job applicants, your role of interest and experience

We do not use cookies, tracking pixels, or third-party analytics on this website, and we do not collect any personal information automatically as you browse.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide solely to:

  • Respond to your enquiry about our services
  • Process and respond to job applications
  • Contact you regarding a quote, site assessment, or employment opportunity

We do not use your information for marketing unrelated to your original enquiry, and we do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to any third party.

How We Store and Protect Your Information

Enquiries and applications submitted through this website are processed through our website hosting provider's secure form-handling service and are accessible only to authorized DGuardian personnel. We take reasonable steps to protect this information from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure.

Data Retention

We retain enquiry and application information only for as long as necessary to respond to your request or, in the case of job applications, for as long as reasonably useful for future hiring consideration, unless you ask us to delete it sooner.

Your Rights

You have the right to ask us what personal information we hold about you, to request corrections, or to request that we delete your information. To make such a request, please contact us at dguardiansales@gmail.com or call 8178473667.

Third-Party Links

Our website may contain links to external sites, such as maps or client websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these external sites, and we encourage you to review their policies separately.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for legal and regulatory reasons. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this privacy policy or how your information is handled, please reach out to us at dguardiansales@gmail.com, or write to us at 61B Shop, Pocket A3, Mayur Vihar Phase 3, Delhi - 110096.
