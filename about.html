About DGuardian

Built on 25 years of on-ground experience.

Incorporated in 2022, but built on discipline and relationships going back a quarter century.

DGuardian Solutions Pvt. Ltd. was incorporated on 30 August 2022, but the expertise behind it goes back much further. Our founder brings over 25 years of hands-on experience in security, staffing and facility management, and built DGuardian to bring that same discipline into a fully compliant, transparent operation.

What started as a single-founder operation has grown into a team deploying 350-500 trained personnel across Delhi NCR, serving schools, corporate offices, industrial units, hospitals, malls, and residential communities including RWAs. Roughly 80% of our clients have stayed with us since the company's earliest days, a number we take as our real report card.

We built DGuardian around a simple idea: a security and staffing partner should be easy to verify, not just easy to trust. That means police-verified staff with documents shared directly with clients, monthly check-ins instead of silence, and a founder who still picks up the phone when something needs fixing.

We're fully licensed and compliant: PSARA-licensed in Uttar Pradesh, Delhi and Haryana, and registered under GST, MSME, Labour License, and EPF/ESIC, so every engagement is on solid legal footing from day one.
